Installing GridSnap
===================

1. Drag GridSnap into the Applications folder (in this window).

2. Open GridSnap from Applications.
   macOS will say it can't verify the developer. That's expected: GridSnap
   isn't distributed through Apple. Click "Done" (not "Move to Bin").

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the
   "Security" section and click "Open Anyway" next to GridSnap.
   Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click "Open Anyway" again.

   You only need to do this once.

4. GridSnap will ask for Accessibility access, which it needs to move
   windows. Click "Open System Settings" and turn GridSnap on.

That's it. GridSnap lives in the menu bar (the grid icon at the top right
of your screen). Click it and choose "Settings…" to set up your grid and
shortcuts.

Default shortcuts (Option + Command + ...):
  1  Top left         2  Top right
  4  Bottom left      3  Bottom right
  5  Centre           6  Centre, full height
  E  Full screen

Control + Option + Command + H turns Dim Background on and off.

Updating: quit GridSnap from its menu, then drag the new version into
Applications and replace the old one. You shouldn't need to grant access again.
